About Our Rescue

WHO WE ARE

Every Animal Deserves Love and Protection.

Domestic Animal Rescue Group (DARG) is a non-profit animal rescue organisation based in Hout Bay, Cape Town.

We are dedicated to rescuing, protecting and caring for abandoned, neglected and abused cats and dogs. Our goal is to give every animal a second chance by providing care, love and the opportunity to find a forever home.

OUR STORY

We Give Cats and Dogs a Second Chance!

Domestic Animal Rescue Group (DARG) was established to help animals that have been abandoned, neglected or abused.

Over the years, DARG has continued to rescue, care for, sterilise and find loving homes for cats and dogs.

The organisation also supports surrounding communities, including Imizamo Yethu and Hangberg, by providing animal welfare services and promoting responsible pet care.

The Rush Ndou DARG Community Clinic was opened to provide affordable veterinary care to animals in these communities.

Today, DARG continues working towards improving the lives of animals through rescue, rehabilitation, education and community support.

WHAT WE BELIEVE IN

Our Mission & Vision

Our work is guided by compassion, kindness and the belief that every animal deserves a better life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to care for animals in need and give them a second chance at life.

We rescue, sterilise, rehabilitate and rehome abused, neglected and abandoned cats and dogs.

We aim to provide every animal with care, protection and love while finding them safe and loving homes.

Our Vision

Our vision is for every adoptable animal at DARG to find a safe, caring and loving home.

We aim to promote the well-being of companion animals and create a community where animals are treated with kindness, respect and compassion.
